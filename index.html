About FreeVerse

A student-first freelancing marketplace built around learning, opportunity and real work.

🎯 Our Vision

To create a practical space where student talent meets real project opportunities and every small project can become a portfolio milestone.

🚀 Our Mission

Make the first step into freelancing easier for students while giving clients a clear way to discover emerging talent.

💙 Why FreeVerse?

  • 🎓 Student-focused freelancer profiles
  • 🌍 Foreign-client-friendly USD workflow
  • 💼 Real project posting and applications
  • 🤝 Separate student and client accounts
  • 🧩 Skills, LinkedIn and GitHub profiles
  • 📈 Portfolio and practical experience building
  • 🔐 Simple prototype with clear account roles
  • ✨ Clean, responsive and beginner-friendly interface
